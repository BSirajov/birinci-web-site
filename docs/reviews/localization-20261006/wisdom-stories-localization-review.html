Editorial Review and Suggestions

6 October 2026 · Review of 256 prose stories · Five poems intentionally excluded

Most translations are clear and faithful. The collection would benefit from specific corrections and a careful native-speaker review, rather than a complete rewrite.

This report explains every issue identified in the review, shows the relevant wording, and suggests how to address it. Suggestions are for discussion; no stories have been changed. The review is AI-assisted and does not replace a native literary editor’s judgment.

Recommended approach

  1. Clarify the original first. Resolve the questions below and agree on spellings of names and explanations of cultural expressions.
  2. Correct meaning and comprehension. Give first attention to reversed instructions, missing actions, unclear people, misleading idioms and altered emotional meaning.
  3. Arrange native-speaker review. Include long stories, dialogue, religious expressions and wordplay. Ask the editor to read the translation independently, then compare it with Azerbaijani.
  4. Polish only where needed. Improve awkward wording without changing passages that already read naturally.
  5. Review manageable batches. Work through about twenty stories at a time, keeping a record of proposed changes and checking each title and moral carefully.
  6. Approve before publication. Update the website and any affected illustration text only after the revised wording is agreed.

Native Kyrgyz review is especially valuable for negation and figurative expressions; Russian for cultural formulas and dialogue; English for idiom and emotional phrasing; German for historical names, wordplay and relationship nuance.
