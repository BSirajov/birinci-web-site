Background options

Two directions for an educational platform: warm & bright, or minimal & clean. Each card shows a hero mock and a recurring page mock so you can compare tone and fit.

Warm & bright

Morning library light

Sun through tall windows onto quiet desks. Credible and inviting; low detail so type stays clear.

  • Best: Homepage hero, Stories home
  • Also: Root language gate (softened)
Warm & bright

Open notebook & soft shadow

Warm desk light, shallow focus, human scale without faces. Feels studious, not childish.

  • Best: Story list, category headers
  • Avoid as: Full-site fixed background (too busy)
Warm & bright

Campus path in soft sun

Tree-lined walk, distant figures only. Speaks to students and adult learners without looking like a university brochure.

  • Best: About / mission, root entry
  • Also: Soft hero if library feel is too “bookish”
Warm & bright

Paper + sky (abstract)

Cream paper dissolving into pale blue. Ideal as the quiet texture behind all pages.

  • Best: Site-wide body background
  • Pair with: A photographic hero on home only
Warm & bright

Hands turning a page

Respectful, cropped, intergenerational. Builds trust for mission copy; use sparingly.

  • Best: About / mission panel, Discoveries intro
  • Not for: Every page background
Minimal & clean

Pale paper grain

Near-white fiber texture. Almost invisible — keeps long reading sessions calm.

  • Best: Global site background, category pages
  • Pair with: Strong photo only on the home hero
Minimal & clean

Soft horizon gradient

Foggy sea/sky edge in muted blue-gray. Modern and open; fits Discoveries as well as home.

  • Best: Homepage hero (minimal path), Discoveries hub
  • Also: Soft full-bleed under root sections
Minimal & clean

Single object, lots of air

One pearl / book / compass in negative space. Brand-adjacent without repeating the logo loudly.

  • Best: Section dividers, empty states
  • Also: About sidebar visual
Minimal & clean

Architectural light

Window grid and soft shadow geometry. Trustworthy and contemporary without glass-tower corporate chill.

  • Best: About, Discoveries article chrome
  • Tone: Credible, slightly cooler than library
Minimal & clean

Quiet desk from above

Cool daylight, few props. Feels like a focused study space for older students and adults.

  • Best: Framing around tools / list view
  • Also: Secondary hero on Discoveries

Recommended combinations

Hybrid (suggested)

Hero: Morning library (warm)
Site texture: Pale paper grain (minimal)

Approachable first impression, clean reading everywhere else.

All warm

Hero: Campus path
Site texture: Paper + sky

Softer, more human — good if the brand should feel hospitable first.

All minimal

Hero: Soft horizon
Site texture: Pale paper grain

More “knowledge platform” — still warm via soft blues, not corporate gray.
