For those who have reached sixty...

Text

It is time to use the money you have saved up so far. Use it and enjoy it. For those who are not aware of the sacrifices you have made to get it, do not save it. This is the ideal time to enjoy peace.

Do not worry about the financial situation of your children and grandchildren, and do not be upset that you have spent your money on yourself. You have taken care of them for years, taught them everything you could. You have given them education, food, shelter and support. Now it is their duty to earn their own money. Let them find their own way.

Now lead a healthy life without much physical exertion. Exercise moderately, go for a walk every day, eat well and sleep well.

At this age, it is easy to get sick and difficult to stay healthy. Therefore, you should keep yourself in good shape, pay attention to your medical and physical needs. Contact your doctor; get check-ups even if you feel fine.

Do not stress over small things. You have already overcome a lot in your life. You have had both good and bad memories. Don't let the past hold you back and the future scare you. Feel good now. Minor troubles will soon be forgotten.

Regardless of your age, always live your love. Love your partner, love life, love your family and remember: as long as your mind and compassion are in order, you are not old.

Don't stop going to the hairdresser, take care of your nails, visit a dermatologist and dentist, use perfumes and creams. When you are well-groomed on the outside, this feeling also seeps into you, you feel proud and strong.

Don't lose sight of age-appropriate fashion trends, but maintain your own style. Read books, flip through newspapers, watch the news. Go online, find out what people think. Make sure you have an active email account and use at least a few social networks.

Respect the younger generation and their opinions. They may not agree with you, but the future is theirs and they will direct their lives in the direction they want. Do not criticize, give advice and try to remind them that yesterday's wisdom is still valid today.

Never use the phrase "in our time...". Now is your time. Be a part of this time as long as you live.

Try to stay away from negative people and spend your time only with people who give you a good mood, positive energy.

Do not give up your hobbies. You can travel, walk, cook, read, dance. If you want, you can keep a cat or a dog.

Get out of the house, meet people you have not seen for a long time, experience something new or old anew. The main thing is to get out of the house from time to time. Go to museums, walk in the park.

Try to always be polite, do not complain and criticize unless necessary. If someone has broken your heart, forgive them. Apologize to the people you have broken your heart to.

Don't carry your pain with you. It will only hurt you. It doesn't matter who is right. They say that holding a grudge is like poisoning yourself and waiting for someone else to die. Never accept that poison. Forgive, forget and move on with your life.

Laugh, be happy. Enjoy a peaceful life at this age of your life... Be happy!

Moral: The era of self-sacrifice will end one day; in the autumn of your life, give yourself the right to live. A happy person spreads happiness to those around him.

What has changed after sixty

Text

I asked one of my friends, who is in her 60s and approaching 70, what changes she has noticed in herself. She replied:

I stopped telling the elderly, “You told me that story.” After all, these conversations help them revive their good memories and relive those wonderful moments.

I learned not to correct people even when they make mistakes. After all, it is not my job to make everyone perfect. Being present is more important than perfection.

I give a lot of compliments. After all, this is pleasant not only to the person in front of me, but also to my own soul.

I learned not to worry about a wrinkle or a stain on my clothes. Ultimately, a person’s value comes from within, not from their appearance.

I learned to stay away from people who are strangers to my world. After all, they are not able to establish and maintain good relationships; and I learned that too.

I learned not to be ashamed of my feelings. After all, it is my feelings that make me human.

I learned that it is better to put my ego aside than to destroy relationships. After all, ego leads me to loneliness, and relationships help me not to be lonely.

I learned to do things that can make me happy. After all, I am responsible for my own happiness.

I learned not to help without being asked. After all, a person who needs help may not want this help to come from me.

I learned not to complain about my health, my neighbors, my relatives, my retirement! After all, despite all the difficulties, I must be grateful that I am alive and that my loved ones are with me.

I stopped saying phrases like “when I was your age…” to young people; and I never use expressions like “I gave you my best years…”. After all, every time has its own rules, and it is not right to apply the criteria of the past to the present.

I began to pay more attention to my wife, not forgetting that she was once young, strong, and full of joy — even though she is now a wrinkled, helpless, and sad old woman. After all, maybe she is the only person who really needs me right now.

I stopped blaming myself for everything. After all, I have done everything I can in life for myself and for my children.

And finally: I learned to ask my Lord for strength to change the things I can, patience to change the things I cannot, and wisdom to know the difference between the two. After all, as a human being, I am not capable of everything.

Moral: Age teaches a person the greatest wisdom: peace is better than perfection, and understanding is better than being right.
