Our site presents rich information about humanity’s magnificent achievements throughout history in science and technology, the natural sciences, the social and human sciences, as well as literature and the arts; about universal moral values, outstanding figures, and important historical discoveries and inventions. Our aim is to preserve this valuable heritage gathered over the centuries, to systematize it, and to pass it on to future generations as a source of knowledge, moral lesson, and inspiration.

Stories are taken from open Internet sources; illustrations were created with AI.
